## Science Outreach and Engagement

I am strongly interested in communicating science. My outreach activities so far have focused on education, mentoring, and participation in scientific and cultural exchange programs.

### Academic and Educational Outreach


- **Erasmus+ Mobility Programme (2022–2023)** — Le Mans Université, France  
  During my Erasmus stay, in addition to academic coursework, I took part in a cultural exchange initiative in which I taught Spanish to local students.
  
- **Poster Presentation**  
  *Strong parameter hierarchy in astrochemistry*  
  XXV Congreso de Física Estadística (FISES’25), Santiago de Compostela (2025).
  Presented a poster on the results of my JAE Intro research internship at the Centro de Astrobiología (CAB), CSIC-INTA. 

### Broader Interests

Beyond formal outreach activities, I have a strong interest in interdisciplinary dialogue between physics, biology, and philosophy, particularly regarding questions related to the **emergence of complexity** and **the origins of life**. I am motivated to expand my outreach efforts in the future through public talks, workshops, and educational initiatives aimed at non-specialist audiences.
