## Science Outreach and Engagement

I am strongly interested in communicating science beyond the academic community, particularly in the areas of **complex systems**, **physics**, and **the origins of life**. My outreach activities so far have focused on education, mentoring, and participation in scientific and cultural exchange programs.

### Academic and Educational Outreach

- **University Tutoring Program (2021–2022)**  
  I participated in the *Programa de Tutores* at the University of Santiago de Compostela, a scheme aimed at supporting first-year university students and facilitating their integration into academic life.

### Broader Interests

Beyond formal outreach activities, I have a strong interest in interdisciplinary dialogue between physics, biology, and philosophy, particularly regarding questions related to the **emergence of complexity** and **the origins of life**. I am motivated to expand my outreach efforts in the future through public talks, workshops, and educational initiatives aimed at non-specialist audiences.
